The story so far

Same brand. Same pick. Better pakora.

Pick a Pakora is an Islamabad-based food brand, owned and managed locally. We were the first outlet in Islamabad to offer a full menu of pakora varieties under one name. The first outlet did well. The building did not.

  1. 2016

    Opened at Safa Gold Mall

    3 March 2016, 7th floor food court, Jinnah Super. Strong response from customers, referrals and mall management from the first week.

  2. 2016

    Grew fast

    Lipton partnered with us for tea. Today's Special became a daily habit for regulars. We took the brand to Footy Mania '16.

  3. Later

    The food court closed

    CDA shut down the Safa Gold food court. The outlet closed with it, for reasons that had nothing to do with the business.

  4. 2026

    Relaunch at Giga Mall

    New identity, new menu, reworked recipes, and a marketing plan built for how Islamabad eats today.

What's new this time

Built for a brand-conscious city.

Islamabad customers expect the whole experience, not just good food. We are not reopening the old kiosk. We rebuilt the brand.

New identity

A redesigned logo, colour system and packaging that work on a signboard, a paper bag and a delivery app listing.

Bigger menu

Nine pakora varieties, kachori in three sizes, wraps, an Afghani burger, house bakery and craft flavoured sodas.

Better taste

Home chefs with 10 years of experience who care about hygiene. Recipes standardised so every order is the same, and veg and chicken never share oil.

Digital-first marketing

Instagram, TikTok, delivery platforms and a loyalty programme, alongside the in-mall activity that worked in 2016.

Today's Special, every day.

A different pakora recipe on the chalkboard each day was the most popular thing about the Safa Gold outlet. Egg pakora, baingan, zucchini, spaghetti pakora, banana pakora, daal kachori combos. It returns in the months after the Giga Mall opening, once the core menu is running smoothly, and goes on Instagram every morning.

Proof we've done this before

The Safa Gold outlet, 2016.

Product, packaging, the kiosk and the customer feedback board. New photography of the relaunch menu follows once the kitchen is set up. Since pakora is carry-and-eat food, we are also introducing a friendly, unique disposable plate and holder that keeps pakoras and chutneys together in one hand.

Launch and marketing

Ready to move the day a unit opens up.

Brand, menu and suppliers are in place. The plan below starts the moment space is confirmed.

Four weeks before opening

  • "We're back" teaser campaign on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, telling the 2016 story
  • Flyers and standees across the mall
  • Delivery platform listings set up before day one
  • Soft opening for mall management, influencers and press

Launch week

  • Bunting, standees and in-mall announcements
  • Opening offers and a free Today's Special sampling counter
  • Loyalty card launch: every tenth order free

Year one

  • Promotions around New Year, Valentine's Day, 23 March, Ramzan and Eid, Independence Day and the mall anniversary
  • Seasonal menu changes; winter and monsoon campaigns when pakora demand peaks
  • Daily social content with paid boosts around offers
  • Participation in mall and community events, as with Footy Mania in 2016
Launch and marketing budgets will be finalised and shared once space availability is confirmed, so they can be allocated against the actual location, size and opening date.

Our request

A place in the queue for the next food court unit.

We understand no space is available right now. We'd like to be placed in line for the next suitable unit at Giga Mall, and we're ready to move quickly once one is offered. One requirement: as a frying outlet we need a unit with a proper exhaust, so oil heat and smoke are vented out of the food court.
